Keyword definitions:

- Staffing:
  Applies when the report involves inadequate staffing levels, staff absence, improper staff-to-youth ratios, lack of supervision due to staffing, unqualified staff coverage, or staff failing to monitor youth because of staffing/supervision problems.

- Youth-on-youth violence:
  Applies when the report involves physical violence, assault, fighting, battery, threats with physical aggression, or serious intimidation by one youth against another youth.

- Staff-on-youth violence:
  Applies when the report involves physical violence, assault, battery, excessive force, or inappropriate physical aggression by staff against a youth.

- Restraint:
  Applies when the report involves physical, mechanical, or chemical restraint of a youth, including improper, excessive, undocumented, unauthorized, or unsafe restraint.

- Staff-on-youth verbal abuse:
  Applies when the report involves staff yelling at, insulting, threatening, humiliating, degrading, cursing at, or verbally mistreating a youth.

- Administrative:
  Applies only to paperwork, documentation, recordkeeping, reporting, notification, licensing, training-log, policy/procedure, or other compliance failures that do not themselves directly describe harm, threatened harm, deficient supervision, deficient care, abuse, neglect, violence, restraint, seclusion, medical issues, education issues, runaway incidents, sexual misconduct, or hazardous physical conditions.

  Mark "yes" only when the report contains a finding about an administrative or paperwork failure as its own violation, such as:
  - missing, incomplete, inaccurate, late, or falsified records
  - failure to document required checks, incidents, treatment plans, grievances, medication records, education records, staff credentials, background checks, or training records
  - failure to notify, report, file, submit, review, sign, approve, or maintain required documents
  - failure to follow licensing or procedural requirements where the issue is primarily documentation or process-based

  Mark "no" when the violation directly concerns youth care, safety, supervision, abuse, neglect, restraint, seclusion, medical care, education, runaway prevention, sexual misconduct, staffing levels, or physical hazards, even if the facility also failed to follow a rule or policy.

  Mark "uncertain" only when the text describes a process or policy failure but it is unclear whether the failure was merely paperwork/compliance-related or directly affected youth care or safety.

- Seclusion:
  Applies when the report involves isolating, confining, locking, or placing a youth alone in a room or area away from others, especially as discipline, control, or crisis management.

- Medical neglect:
  Applies when the report involves failure to provide, arrange, document, administer, or follow up on medical, dental, mental health, medication, emergency, or other health-related care needed by a youth.

- Educational neglect:
  Applies when the report involves failure to provide required schooling, educational services, special education services, school attendance, instruction, or educational access.

- Runaway:
  Applies when the report involves a youth running away, escaping, eloping, leaving without authorization, being absent without leave, or staff failing to prevent or respond to such an incident.

- Youth-to-youth sexual misconduct:
  Applies when the report involves sexual abuse, assault, harassment, coercion, inappropriate sexual contact, exposure, exploitation, or sexually inappropriate behavior by one youth toward another youth.

- Staff-to-youth sexual misconduct:
  Applies when the report involves sexual abuse, assault, harassment, coercion, grooming, exploitation, inappropriate sexual contact, sexual comments, or boundary violations by staff toward a youth.

- Hazardous physical environment:
  Applies when the report involves unsafe physical conditions, broken fixtures, exposed hazards, unsafe rooms, dangerous materials, sanitation hazards, fire hazards, security hazards, or environmental risks in the facility.
